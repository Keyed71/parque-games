How it works
  • Each round shows one survey question with 10 hidden answers (worth 100 points in total).
  • The game runs for as many rounds as there are teams, so every team starts a round once — no first-mover advantage.
  • On their turn a team keeps guessing until their guesses run out. Click a board answer to reveal it.
  • Not on the board? That guess is used up and the next team gets one free steal guess. Then play returns to the original team if they have guesses left.
