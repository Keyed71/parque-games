How it works
  • Each round shows one survey question with 10 hidden answers (worth 100 points in total).
  • The game runs for as many rounds as there are teams, so every team starts a round once — no first-mover advantage.
  • Teams take turns one guess at a time (Team 1, Team 2, Team 1…) — three guesses each. Click a board answer to reveal it.
  • Not on the board? That guess is used up and the next team gets one free bonus guess — then turns carry on as normal.
